### Key regression numbers (DR-001 outcome, from the committed tests)

- M(q) planar restriction vs M2 SymPy reference: max abs diff **3.5e-18** (100 configs).
- RNEA bias vs energy-derived Jacobian bias: **5.8e-14**.
- Planar trajectory regression vs M2 reduced model: **< 1e-6** over 2 s
  (x, z, R(θ) compared as matrices, α) — `test_planar_limit_recovers_reduced_model`.
- Gravity-free 1 s tumble: Δp_lin **4.1e-16**, ΔKE **2.2e-13** rel, ΔL < 1e-9.
- Gravity free fall: p(t) = p0 + m·g·t to < 1e-9.
- Quaternion norm deviation at accepted steps: < 1e-12 (renormalized each step);
  dense-output sample max dev 6.5e-13 over the hover run.
- Clamped chain ≡ equivalent composite single body: identical to 8+ digits.

The cases differ in the expected direction and magnitude ordering; the dominant

### Free-body hover run (placeholder parameters, NOT trim-tuned)

Over 0.2 s (3 wingbeats): sinks 0.114 m (mean aero lift 0.067 N < 0.147 N
weight — untrimmed placeholders), drifts forward 0.045 m, pitches down past
90° (physical pitch instability without feedback; the Euler yaw/roll jumps in
the plot are ZYX branch-flip display artifacts — the quaternion state is
smooth). Locked-joint reaction torque carries the wingbeat loading
(±5.8 mN·m).

### Performance (measured, this machine)

Per plant evaluation: 16.9 ms → **4.1 ms** after hot-path optimization
(per-wing shared kinematic context + fast 3-vector cross products). A 0.2 s
free-body run at 200 steps/wingbeat costs ~140 s wall. Recorded as a known
cost; optimization is not a correctness gate.

## 3. Known failures and limitations

- **Abdomen aerodynamics: stub only** — `abdomen_aero_enabled: true` raises
  `NotImplementedError` (Level 3, brief §6.5).
- **Added-mass term excludes body accelerations** (A-009; algebraic-loop
  avoidance). Fluid added mass folded into M(q) would be the consistent fix —
  deferred.
- **Wings are massless**; flapping-actuator reaction torques on the thorax are
  not modelled (A-004). Aero acts as point forces on the thorax only.
- Joint-limit stops in `torque_driven` are compliant penalties with
  placeholder stiffness/damping (modelling choice, labelled).
- The X-wing draft keeps the aft pair in the same orientation (anti-phase
  stroke only); true fore/aft geometry mirroring awaits CAD-derived geometry
  (DR-004).
- Servo dynamics + power (stage 4, brief §6.4): deferred.
- Leng et al. numerical reproduction: **PENDING** (no original data).

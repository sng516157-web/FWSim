## 3. The T8 bug narrative (worth recording)

The closed-loop demo diverged repeatedly (theta past 4000 rad). Each failure
peeled one layer; all fixes are regression-pinned:

1. **`tau_act_trim` sign error** — the actuator torque holding the abdomen at
   trim must equal `G[3] = −m_a g l_a cos(θ+α)`; the initial implementation
   had the opposite sign, making the "trim" a non-equilibrium.
2. **Missing x-force balance** — the trim solved only lift + moment; the
   residual forward force (≈1.25 mN) is not an equilibrium either. Fixed by
   adding θ_trim as a third trim variable. Verified: trim is an exact
   equilibrium of the nonlinear plant (`Q − G = 0` to 1e-8).
3. **Control-channel units mismatch** — LQR treats 1 N m as one unit of
   effort; the trim torque is 5.9e-4 N m and the abdomen inertia ~1e-6, so
   unscaled gains detonated the plant (eigenvalue at −2.5e6, accelerations
   ~1e4 rad/s²). Fixed by channel scaling (`control_scale`).
4. **The physical one:** with (1)-(3) fixed, the *linear* closed loop
   converged but the *nonlinear* plant diverged from every perturbation,
   including the open loop — the trim was **open-loop unstable in the
   nonlinear plant**. Cause: a constant cycle-averaged force removes the
   wings' dominant hover-stabilizing mechanism — aerodynamic body damping
   (flapping counter-torque + heave damping). The α→θ̈ mass coupling pumps
   the near-neutral pitch mode until the abdomen gravity well tips.
   **Fix:** `dynamics/aero_damping.py` computes the damping derivatives
   (dFz/dvz = −3.8e-2 N/(m/s), dMy/dωy = −8.95e-5 N m/(rad/s) — ~90× the
   structural joint damping) from the same time-resolved blade-element
   computation the mean forces come from (A-007 chain), injected as a
   generalized damping matrix into both plant and design linearization.
   With it, the loop holds a 0.2 rad perturbation with bounded control.

Also recorded: the *unbounded* trim solve folds the abdomen to α ≈ π — a
mathematically valid but physically absurd, unstable equilibrium. α is now
bounded to the joint limits and the moment is closed by the wing-placement
shift `x_T_shift` (−8 mm for the bio placeholder geometry — i.e., trim says
the wings must sit at the CoM, a real design output).

The lesson that generalizes (noted for M7): a cycle-averaged plant model
needs cycle-averaged *derivatives* too, not just mean forces — the mean is
necessary but not sufficient for dynamics.

## 4. Known limitations

- Wake-capture / clap-and-fling gains are `estimated` placeholders (sysid
  targets); the reduced forms are engineering proxies, not resolved unsteady
  models (validity warning fires when active).
- The control loop is a Level-1 design demo (cycle-averaged forces, A-007),
  not a flight autopilot; free-body (Level-2) trim is future work.
- Sysid fits scale parameters by default; shape parameters
  (cl_freq/phase etc.) are poorly identified by force-only data (documented).
- A validated-against-hardware vehicle remains open pending the user's rig data.

## 2. Cross-validation result (Task 2) — the honest negative

Three-way on the bio hover case (60° amplitude, no dihedral, 15 Hz):

| model | mean Fz (N, both wings) |
|---|---|
| quasi-steady blade element (ours) | −0.1377 |
| UVLM (ptera, prescribed wake) | −0.0018 |

**UVLM underpredicts hover lift by ~76×.** Interpretation: at insect/hover
Re, force production is LEV/viscous-dominated, which inviscid potential-flow
UVLM does not contain. This answers the M4 follow-up: **wake-induction
physics (UVLM) does NOT close the AirPulse gap** — the gap is in the
viscous/regime layer, not the potential layer. The forward-flight anchor
(2 m/s, zero flap) behaves sanely (near-zero lift at zero AoA, small induced
drag), confirming the adapter is not broken.

Consequences (recorded):
- UVLM is a valid tier for FORWARD-flight / high-advance-ratio studies, not
  hover at our Re. The ladder now states this.
- The AirPulse clap regime needs Navier–Stokes (rVPM/CFD) or the reduced
  unsteady terms (M6 T7) — UVLM is excluded by its movement range anyway.

## 3. Passive torsional wing pitch (Task 3)

`dynamics/wing_pitch_compliance.py` + `WingSpec.pitch_spring` +
`experiment.passive_pitch`: per-wing torsional spring-damper DOF replacing
the prescribed pitch profile (AirPulse's passive feathering). Fixed-body
runner steps [psi, psi_dot] per sprung wing with the aero pitching moment
from the batched blade-element evaluation.

Integration finding (measured, not assumed): an adaptive solve_ivp
(DOP853/LSODA/RK45) stalls — the aero RHS is non-smooth enough that error
control grinds at ~5e-7 s steps (~1.7 ms/eval → hours per wingbeat). An
explicit constant-M step diverges because the measured aero pitch stiffness
(~0.15 N m/rad, restorative) exceeds any soft spring. Shipped solution:
**semi-implicit exact integrator** — the aero moment is finite-differenced
each step, folded into the linearized 2x2 oscillator, and propagated by
matrix exponential. Unconditionally stable at wingbeat-rate steps; 1
wingbeat in 0.15 s.

Physics check captured in tests: springs softer than the aero stiffness
(k < ~0.15 N m/rad on AirPulse) diverge torsionally — k=2e-5 blows up,
k=0.05 finds a stable ±3.3 rad limit cycle, k=0.5 settles to ~10 mrad.
Tests: stiff limit holds rest (k=1e2 → ~2e-5 rad); moderate spring bounded
and settled; missing block errors.

Example end-to-end: `experiments/m7_airpulse_passive_pitch.yaml` +
`reference_vehicles/airpulse_passive_pitch.yaml` (k=0.5 N m/rad, estimated).
Run in 6 s; record carries `fidelity_tier: quasi_steady+passive_pitch`;
psi settles to a bounded −11…+3 mrad response. Honest caveat: mean lift is
0.004 N vs 0.252 N weight — with zero rest angle and no prescribed pitch
the wing feathers symmetrically and produces almost no net lift. Passive
feathering on the real robot relies on stroke-plane asymmetry and membrane
stop geometry this reduced model does not capture; the machinery (DOF,
stability, records) is the deliverable, not hover replication.

## 4. Control channels + Gymnasium env (Task 4)

`control/channels.py`: ChannelMixer mapping [thrust, roll, pitch, yaw] onto
per-wing kinematics (Doman et al. 2010 convention; split-cycle as documented
phase approximation). Bug found by test: broadcast kinematics share one
object per side — the mixer now copies per wing.
`control/gym_env.py`: Gymnasium hover env (13-dim obs, 4-channel action,
hover-keeping reward, wingbeat-scale steps); `check_env` passes; zero-action
rollout finite.

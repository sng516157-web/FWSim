## 4. Known limitations

- The 3D panel requires a human to verify browser rendering (MeshCat in
  QWebEngineView); offscreen Qt can't render WebEngine content.
- Matrix3Parameter fields are read-only in the editor (edit inertia tensors in
  YAML); noted in the form.
- Qt signal delivery without an event loop is queued — the workflow test calls
  `processEvents()` explicitly (test-only concern; the real GUI runs the loop).
- No live-update during runs (stage file: optional); plots load at completion.
  Post-run 3D playback (play/pause, frame slider, speed) was added after M5
  (see `ui/scene3d.py`); during-run streaming remains out of scope.

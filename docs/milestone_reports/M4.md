### Pipeline proof (synthetic known-truth, `datasets/synthetic/hover_case`)

- Known phase offset 0.012 s → recovered 0.01188 s (**0.05% of a wingbeat**;
  clean-sinusoid unit test recovers to < 0.1%: 6.7e-5 s bound at 15 Hz; the
  noisy short-window roundtrip achieves ~0.2%, documented).
- NRMSE **0.012** — within the known injected noise level (2%); Pearson
  **0.9995**; phase lag at the dominant harmonic ≈ 0.
- `validation_status: SYNTHETIC_PROOF` — pipeline-only, never a validation
  claim.

### AirPulse literature rig comparison (coarse, point values)

Condition: their flapping rig (still air, 3.25 Hz, ±70° stroke, phase-averaged
coefficients per their Eqs. 15–18; our run filtered with their 4th-order
50 Hz Butterworth spec).

| Coefficient | Paper (4-vein full wing) | Ours | Rel. residual |
|---|---|---|---|
| C_Fx (axial) | 6.084 | 0.071 | −0.99 |
| C_Fz (vertical) | 4.780 | 1.408 | −0.70 |

**This comparison FAILS, recorded honestly** (brief §11.4). Interpretation
caveats (not excuses): (a) the paper's normalization/assembly mapping
(per-assembly vs whole-robot) is ambiguous from the text — their coefficients
imply rig forces above the robot's weight; (b) our pitch kinematics are a
placeholder for their passive feathering; (c) our Dickinson coefficients are
Robofly-regime while AirPulse runs at Re ~ 2e4–1e5; (d) our wings are
massless, theirs dominate the inertia (38% of mass); (e) no clap-and-fling or
compliance in our model. Resolving which factor dominates requires the paper's
raw time series or author clarification — the comparison machinery is ready
for either.

## 3. Known failures and limitations

- The AirPulse rig comparison fails by 70–99% (above) — recorded, not tuned away.
- The synthetic pipeline proof validates *machinery*, not physics.
- No experimental validation dataset exists for any vehicle in this project;
  every data-dependent validation item is skipped with that reason.
- The `estimate_offset` phase offset is modulo one wingbeat (integer-cycle
  ambiguity, stated in every comparison report).
- filtfilt edge transients are documented; comparisons should trim edges.

## 4. Deferred items

- Parameter fitting / system identification (infrastructure records
  provenance; fitting itself is out of M4 scope per the stage file).
- Wing-mass bodies in the dynamics model (the top fidelity gap for AirPulse;
  also noted in the M3 report).
- `fwmav-sim compare` against the user's future rig data: importer + manifest
  are ready.
